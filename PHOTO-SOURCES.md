# Image provenance

The three hotlinked photographs and five unused public JPEGs were removed because no reusable license or authorization was established. Attribution and public availability do not establish reuse rights.

`public/images/park-illustration.svg` is an original geometric composition created for this guide and dedicated under CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . It is not a photograph, site plan, depiction of the sculptures or evidence of current facilities. Visible caption and alternative text state that scope.

The existing local logo, favicon and geometric OG artwork remain; they contain abstract leaf/path graphics rather than park photographs. No clearance of depicted property or public-art rights is asserted.
